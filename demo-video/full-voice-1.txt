Every day, we save a little more. A quote from a browser. A link from a tab. A screenshot from a folder. A paragraph from a book. And then, mostly, we don't come back.

The problem isn't capture. It's return.

We save for a future self who never gets the file.

So we built this: a small, private place for the things you mean to keep. One inbox. Not a feed. Not a social profile. Not another place to perform.

Capture from the browser, from Obsidian, or from uTools. A selection, a whole page, a link, an image. Everything lands in the same inbox, with its source still attached. The URL, the title, the site, the moment you saved it.
